        What happens after the shutter

        1. Point it at the plate

          The frame finds the meal or a barcode. Several photos of one meal are fine: the side, the label, the half you already ate.

        2. Say what the camera can’t see

          “With salmon and cream cheese” goes along with the photo. A note is optional; it helps when the sauce hides what is under it.

        3. Three estimates, the middle one counts

          The photo goes to Gemini three times at once, so it takes about as long as once. Kalorat keeps the estimate in the middle: one odd guess can’t move your day.

        4. Every part, in grams

          You see how sure it is, the range it could be, and why. Too much? One tap for lower, one for higher, or change a single part.

        The week, the month, the scale

        Averages that count only the days you logged, a streak that forgives a missed day, and a weight line that shows the trend, not the noise.
